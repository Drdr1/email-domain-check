      What the check looks for

      Authentication

      • SPF: one valid record, the right ending, and the 10-lookup limit most checkers miss
      • Which services you've authorized to send as you
      • DKIM keys for your provider, and their strength
      • DMARC policy, reporting, and Gmail/Yahoo sender rules

      Delivery and reputation

      • MX records and which provider handles your mail
      • Your sending IPs and domain on common blocklists
      • MTA-STS and TLS reporting
      • Domains that don't send mail, locked against spoofing
